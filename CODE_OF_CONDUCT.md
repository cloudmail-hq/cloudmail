# Code of conduct

This applies to cloudmail's issues, discussions, pull requests and any other
place the project is discussed.

## What we expect

- Be respectful, including when you disagree.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Assume the other person means well, and ask before you assume otherwise.
- Keep discussion on the project.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory remarks about anyone's gender, sexuality, race, religion, nationality, age, disability or appearance.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam and repeated off-topic posts.

## Reporting

Email **hello@trycloudmail.com** and say what happened and where. Reports are
kept private. The maintainers may remove comments, lock a thread, or block
someone from the project.
